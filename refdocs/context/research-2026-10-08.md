## A. Financial health forecasting

1. **Use scale-free error metrics.** Percentage errors explode near zero; Hyndman & Koehler recommend MASE/RMSSE. RMSSE was the M5 metric. https://otexts.com/fpp3/accuracy.html [H]
2. **Rolling-origin evaluation** (time-series CV, expanding window) is the standard protocol. https://otexts.com/fpp3/tscv.html [H]
3. **Small-sample forecast comparisons:** the Diebold–Mariano test over-rejects; use the Harvey–Leybourne–Newbold correction. https://eprints.nottingham.ac.uk/43017 [H]. With few origins per series, pool across series and add a Wilcoxon signed-rank test on per-series MASE (Friedman/Nemenyi for ≥4 models).
4. **Banks split fixed and variable flows.** BBVA forecasts recurrent items to a calendar date and non-recurrent spend as category totals. https://bbvaaifactory.com/improving-customer-experience-forecasting-models [H]. Monzo's "left to spend" is the balance minus predicted committed debits. https://monzo.com/blog/2018/08/21/budget-with-monzo [H]. Yodlee's Predicted Cash Flow builds a balance forecast from recurring transactions. https://developer.yodlee.com/api/solutions-apis/predicted-cash-flow [M]
5. **Recurring-transaction detection heuristic:** normalise merchant text → group → dominant inter-arrival gap (7 / 14 / 28–31 days) with low variance → amount within a tolerance band → at least 2–3 occurrences. https://docs.ntropy.com/enrichment/recurrence [M]
6. **Probabilistic balance forecasting in production:** BBVA uses quantile forecasts and raises low-balance alerts on P(negative balance). https://www.bbvaaifactory.com/uncertainty-models-and-detection-of-balance-anomalies/ [H]. Intuit Mint's overdraft early-warning system (KDD '24) cut fees for users. https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.02455 [H]. Bootstrapped-residual simulation for forecast paths: https://otexts.com/fpp3/prediction-intervals.html [H]. Conformal intervals: https://nixtlaverse.nixtla.io/statsforecast/docs/tutorials/conformalprediction.html [H]
7. **Intermittent categories:** Syntetos–Boylan classification (ADI / CV²); Croston / TSB for intermittent spend. [M] — relevant to how the discretionary residual is aggregated (weekly, not daily).
8. **Not adopted as candidates (future work):** ETS/Theta (strong on short series: the Nixtla SCUM ensemble beat Chronos v1 on short monthly data, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.03395 [M]); time-series foundation models — Chronos-2 / Chronos-Bolt (Apache-2.0, CPU-capable, https://github.com/amazon-science/chronos-forecasting [H]), TimesFM 2.5, Moirai 2.0. A 2026 break-even study found foundation models win zero-shot mainly when data is short **and** seasonal. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04919 [M]

## B. BNPL risk classification

1. **Simulation-derived affordability labels have direct precedent.** Bijak, Thomas & Mues, "Dynamic affordability assessment: predicting an applicant's ability to repay over the life of the loan", *Journal of Credit Risk* — simulate income and consumption paths, check repayment at every instalment, define affordability as a probability. https://www.risk.net/journal-of-credit-risk/2335467/dynamic-affordability-assessment-predicting-an-applicants-ability-to-repay-over-the-life-of-the-loan [H] (exact year/volume not confirmed)
2. **BNM's household stress test** uses a borrower "financial margin" (income after deductions − necessities − debt obligations) under simulated shocks; DSR > 60% pre-shock (> 40% post-shock) associates with negative margins. https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2023/03/29/bnm-risk-of-default-low-but-25pc-of-household-borrowers-have-high-service-ratio/62125 [M]
3. **Cash-flow underwriting is predictive** — FinRegLab study of six lenders: cash-flow variables at least as predictive as bureau scores. https://finreglab.org/cash-flow-data-in-underwriting-credit-empirical-research-findings [H]
4. **Rejected label strategies:** expert-rule labels and Snorkel-style weak supervision re-encode the author's formula (v1's failure); transfer from Give Me Some Credit / UCI Taiwan has a population and feature mismatch — sanity-check only. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.17945 [M]
5. **Monotonic constraints cost little:** AUC "price of monotonicity" ≈ 0–2.9% across five public credit datasets (Koklev). https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17945 [H]. Monotone XGBoost beat a WoE scorecard by 7% KS (AAAI 2020). https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/7055 [H]
6. **Counterfactual / recourse explanations:** DiCE (Mothilal, Sharma & Tan, FAT* 2020) https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.07697 [H]; Ustun, Spangher & Liu, "Actionable Recourse in Linear Classification", FAT* 2019, arXiv 1809.06514 [H].
7. **Glass-box argument (context, not adopted):** Rudin 2019, *Nature Machine Intelligence* 1(5) https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.10154 [H]; EBM competitive with boosted trees [M].
8. **Reasons must be specific** — CFPB Circular 2022-03 (US analogue). [H]

## C. Malaysian regulation & market (for grounding and citation)

- **Consumer Credit Act 2025 (Act 873):** gazetted 31 Dec 2025, in force 1 Mar 2026; Consumer Credit Commission regulates non-bank BNPL; providers must assess creditworthiness and affordability incl. existing commitments and DSR — "capacity to fully repay … without undue financial hardship over the duration of the credit facility". https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/personal-finance-news/bnpl-providers-required-to-run-credit-checks-under-consumer-credit-act-2025.html [H]; https://www.dfdl.com/insights/content-hub/articles/malaysia-a-summary-of-the-consumer-credit-act-2025/ [H]. Fee caps: none set yet [M].
- **BNM Policy Document on Personal Financing** (30 Sep 2025; bank and e-money BNPL): repay-in-full assessment; full affordability check (income, indebtedness, prudent DSR) once cumulative BNPL limit ≥ RM1,500; late charges limited to actual costs; account suspended after 3 consecutive missed payments. https://www.skrine.com/insights/alerts/october-2025/bank-negara-malaysia-issues-new-policy-document-on [H]
- **Market (BNM FSR 2H2025, Mar 2026):** RM4.9bn outstanding end-2025; 7.5m active accounts; 2H2025 140.3m transactions / RM11.9bn; overdue 3.2%. https://www.bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2539245 [H]
- **Repayment behaviour (grounding for missed payments):** CCOB task-force study of 21,000 active users — ~88% paid every instalment on time, ~12% late but in full, <0.5% could not repay; 2.6% of accounts overdue at 30 Jun 2025. https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/personal-finance-news/over-168000-bnpl-accounts-had-overdue-balances-as-at-june-2025.html [H]. Earlier FSR: ~44% of users aged 18–30, >80% earned < RM3,000/month. https://www.nst.com.my/amp/business/home/2023/03/894114/growing-missed-repayment-among-bnpl-borrowers [H]
- **Student finances:** PTPTN 2026 max annual loan (public uni, B40) RM6,180 (arts) / RM6,650 (science), paid in 2–3 disbursements (lump-sum income) https://articles.unienrol.com/all-you-need-to-know-about-ptptn/ [M]; Tok & Cheah (2024, IJBF 19(1), N=454) — most students spend RM201–400/month https://e-journal.uum.edu.my/index.php/ijbf/article/view/20396 [H]; DOSM HES 2022 category shares https://www.dosm.gov.my/portal-main/release-content/household-expenditure-survey-report--malaysia--states- [H]. Demographics: Tan et al. (2026, N=150), Osman et al. (2024, N=440) — already in the literature review.
- **Providers 2026** (verify against a real checkout before encoding): SPayLater 1-month 0%, otherwise ~1.5%/month "profit rate", late fee RM10 or principal if lower [M]; Atome pay-in-3 0%, 6/9/12 months 1.5%/month [M]; TikTok PayLater ~1.5% flat [M]; Grab PayLater postpaid or 4/8/12 instalments, RM10 late, age 21+ [H]; Boost PayFlex 2.5%/month + wakalah fee [M]. https://www.stashaway.my/r/best-buy-now-pay-later-malaysia. Detailed billing timing: `bnpl-billing-rules-my.md`.

## D. Product, behaviour & UAT

- **Generic warnings don't change behaviour; personal consequences might.** Central Bank of Ireland experiment: salient disclosures improved understanding but not usage; BNPL raised spend 4.39% vs debit; mental accounting made users feel richer. https://www.centralbank.ie/docs/default-source/publications/research-technical-papers/rtp-15-2025-behavioural-mechanisms-of-bnpl.pdf [H] → show *your* balance with vs without the purchase.
- **Reminders work:** one SMS before the due date cut late-fee incidence by ≥1/5 (AFM/Riverty). https://www.afm.nl/~/profmedia/files/rapporten/2024/bnpl-riverty-experiment-en.pdf [H]
- **Stacking is common:** ~2/3 of US BNPL users had concurrent loans, 1/3 across lenders (CFPB Jan 2025). [H] → cross-provider aggregation.
- **Reflection beats waiting a few minutes:** ~3.5 min listing reasons for/against reduced urge; a 10-minute delay did not (Moser, U. Michigan). [M]
- **Traffic-light labels** were noticed and used more than numeric ones by young adults (calorie-label study; transfer is an inference). [M]
- **Gap check:** no consumer product found (MY or global) combining cross-provider BNPL aggregation + pre-purchase check + balance forecast + explanations. Seedly discontinued 31 Dec 2025 [H]. Finny (US) is closest for aggregation [M].
- **UAT:** SUS average 68, A ≈ 80.8+ (Sauro) https://measuringu.com/interpret-sus-score/ [H]; 5 users find most usability problems (qualitative) https://www.nngroup.com/videos/test-5-users-qual-vs-quant/ [H]; TAM is common in Malaysian student fintech studies [M]. Pre/post decision vignettes (would-you-buy before vs after the check; McNemar) measure decision quality, not just usability.

## E. Tech & privacy

- **Hosting:** Render free = 512 MB / 0.1 CPU, sleeps after 15 min, ~1 min wake [H]. HF Spaces Docker needs PRO; Fly.io has no free tier [M]. Google Cloud Run has an always-free tier with configurable memory [M]. Supabase has a Singapore region; free projects pause after 1 week idle [H/M].
- **Tooling:** uv + Ruff + pytest [H]; Next.js testing per official docs = Vitest + Testing Library, Playwright for async server components [H]; typed client from FastAPI OpenAPI via `openapi-typescript` + `openapi-fetch` (stable, types only) [M].
- **Statement parsing (out of scope, cite only):** `monopoly` (AGPL-3.0, Maybank) https://github.com/benjamin-awd/monopoly [H]; no open parsers found for RHB / Bank Islam / ShopeePay [M].
- **PDPA 2024 amendment:** 72-hour breach notification to the Commissioner, DPO appointment, portability right, cross-border whitelist removed; cross-border transfer guideline 29 Apr 2025 (consent is an exception). https://insightplus.bakermckenzie.com/bm/data-technology/malaysia-personal-data-protection-amendment-act-2024-to-come-into-force [H] → synthetic personas by default; Supabase Singapore = cross-border, cover in the UAT consent form.
- **Optional LLM explanations:** Claude Haiku 4.5 ≈ $0.0025 per explanation [H]. Risks: invented reasons (mitigate: fixed JSON input + number check + template fallback) and PDPA transfer (send derived features only).
