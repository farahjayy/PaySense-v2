# Plan — Persona histories that end in October 2026

> Execution: `refdocs/execution/2026-10-22-persona-histories-oct-2026.md`. Part of P9 (UAT kit); the persona CSVs come from P2 (D-08).

## 1. Why this exists

The UAT is self-guided and starts at the end of October or the beginning of November. The three persona CSVs end on 28 December 2025, so a tester who imports one sees a ledger ten months old and a forecast flagged as out of date. The whole-week shift (`ml.uat.shift_persona`) fixes the staleness but moves a payment on the 1st to some other day of the month and shifts the PTPTN months. The generator already takes an `as_of` day, so the honest fix is to generate the histories for the study period itself.

## 2. What's already covered

- `generate_profile(..., as_of=...)` builds a history ending on any day; `DEFAULT_AS_OF` (28 Dec 2025) is only the default.
- `ml.datagen.personas` picks, deterministically, the first candidate profile that shows what each persona exists to test, and renders the bank-style CSV.
- The tests for import, length, determinism and forecast unlock already run over the persona files.

## 3. Scope

**In**
- A named constant `PERSONA_AS_OF = 2026-10-31`; `build_persona_profile` and `write_personas` take an `as_of` that defaults to it.
- Regenerated `data/personas/*.csv` (five months: 1 June to 31 October 2026).
- A test that the files end in October 2026 and still unlock the forecast.
- A look at what the app says for each regenerated persona (forecast band, risk check on a small and a large purchase), reported as measured.

**Out, with reasons**
- Savings or a buffer in the ledger: the app does not credit savings (D-27); whether to add the savings-buffer input is the open Batch B decision and belongs to the author.
- The self-guided task script and the purchase cards: a separate piece of work.
- Changing the shift tool: it stays as the fallback for a session after the freshness window.
- Any change to `params.yaml` or the generator's logic, so the Engine 2 label provenance hash is unchanged.

## 4. Approach

Keep the seeds. Only the end date changes, so the chosen profile index may differ (the showcase rule runs again); the output is still deterministic. The `.csv` files are regenerated by the module's own command, never hand-edited.

## 5. Risks & unknowns

| Risk | Confidence | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| The forecast calls a ledger stale after 14 days; a tester who starts after 14 November sees the warning | Certain | Say so in the task instructions; the shift tool regenerates a fresher file in a minute |
| Rows dated after the day a tester imports (before 31 October) are ignored by the forecast | High (`service.py` drops future rows) | The study starts after the end date; note it for the author's own trial runs |
| The regenerated profiles show different outcomes from the Dec 2025 ones (the buffer-check numbers came from those) | Medium | Measure and report; re-run the buffer check if the outcomes differ materially |

## 6. Definition of done

- `data/personas/*.csv` regenerate byte-identically from `python -m ml.datagen.personas` and end in October 2026.
- Tests pass, including the new date test and forecast unlock for all three; ruff clean.
- The measured app behaviour on each persona is written in the changelog.
- STATUS and CHANGELOG updated; no schedule language.
