What this is

PaySense is a student project: a website that helps a student think about whether a “buy now, pay later” (BNPL) purchase is a good idea. This session is a user acceptance test. Its purpose is to evaluate how usable the PaySense dashboard is, how useful you find it, and how well its explainable AI features (the parts that show why it gives the answer it does) make sense to you.

This is a test of the website, not of you. There are no right or wrong answers, and nothing you do can break anything. Please do not ask anyone for help or look up how it works. If something is unclear, that is exactly what we want to hear about, so say so in Form B.

Everything here is made up. Use only the details on this page. Please do not type in any of your own bank, e-wallet or BNPL details.

You can stop at any time and skip any question. To have your answers removed, tell the project author your code ({{participant}}) before the report is written. Your name is never written next to your answers.

The imaginary student

For this session you are looking after the money of an imaginary student. Everything you do is for that student, using the practice account and the file above. You do not need to be like them: just do what they would need to do.

What to do

Keep Form B open in a second tab or window as you go, and answer its questions when a step tells you to. Submit it at the end.

Before you open the website

Open Form A, answer it, and submit it. It asks what you think about a purchase before you have seen the website. Then come back here.

Sign in

Open the website and sign in as the student, with the username above.

Get the student’s history in

  • Download the file {{statement_link}}. It contains a few months of this student’s bank statement.
  • Now go back to the PaySense website, and import this file into PaySense.
  • After that, review the student’s transactions in PaySense.

See what PaySense says about the student’s money

  • Go to the Dashboard in PaySense and explore it: look at what it tells you about this student’s money over the next few months.
  • When you have finished exploring, open Form B and write, in your own words, what you got from the dashboard.

Tell PaySense about the regular monthly payments

Like most people, this student has payments that repeat every month.

  • Go to the Recurring page in PaySense and confirm every suggestion it makes.

Tell PaySense the student’s BNPL limit

The student has a {{provider}} account with a limit of {{limit}}.

  • Go to the Plans page in PaySense and tell it about this limit.

Check three purchases

The student is thinking about the {{card_count}} purchases below. Check each one on its own, as if it were the only purchase the student is making. Go in the order shown.

  • Go to the Risk check page in PaySense.
  • For each purchase, type in exactly what is on the card.
  • After every risk check, read the result and analyse it: what PaySense tells you, and why.
  • If PaySense suggests a way to make the purchase safer, take the suggestion and try it.
  • Then go to Form B and answer its questions for that purchase: what PaySense told you, why you think it said that, what you would do, and what you think of the suggestion. After the first purchase, Form B will also take you back to the scenario from Form A.
  • Do not add any of them to Plans. Nothing is being bought.
