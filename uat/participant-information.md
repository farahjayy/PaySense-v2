# Taking part in a short study of a BNPL decision aid

## What this is

PaySense is a student project that helps people decide whether a "buy now, pay later" (BNPL) purchase is a good idea. It forecasts how your money is likely to look over the coming months and checks how a particular purchase would change that. This session is a user acceptance test. Its purpose is to evaluate how usable the PaySense dashboard is, how useful you find it, and how well its explainable AI features (the parts that show why it gives the answer it does) make sense to you.

## What you will do

About 40 minutes, on your own, on a laptop or a phone:

1. Answer a short question about a purchase, in a form, before you see the website.
2. Use PaySense on a **practice account**, doing a few simple tasks from a page we send you.
3. Answer the same question again, then a few short questions about how it felt to use, in a second form.

## Your data

- The practice account holds **made-up** transactions for an imaginary student. **You do not enter any of your own bank, e-wallet or BNPL details.** Please do not.
- Your answers are kept under a participant code (for example P03). Your name is not written next to your answers.
- Answers are used only for this project's report, together with other people's. The report may quote a short comment without anything that identifies you.
- Your practice account is named after you: your name is the username you sign in with and the name shown on screen. It is kept on a hosted database in Singapore and holds nothing else about you, only the made-up transactions. It will be deleted when the study ends.
- The forms are held by the form service the project author uses. They contain your code and what you choose to write, not your name.

## Your choices

- Taking part is voluntary. You can skip any question and stop at any time without giving a reason, and nothing happens to you if you do.
- You can ask for your answers to be removed at any time before the report is written. Tell the project author your participant code.
- This is a test of the website, not of you. There are no right answers. If something is unclear, that is what we want to find out.
